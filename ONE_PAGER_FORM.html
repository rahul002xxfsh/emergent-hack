IdeaCelebrity Persona Therapist: a voice web app where you vent to a parody celebrity "therapist" and get funny, in-character advice, plus a shareable Session Prescription card.
Idea Breakdown
Product
ProblemStudents and young professionals face small daily stress (deadlines, exams, dating, procrastination) that is too minor for therapy but too heavy to bottle up. Friends get tired of hearing it, and generic AI chatbots reply in the same flat, polite voice, so venting feels like filling in a form. There is no fun way to let it out.
UserCollege students and young professionals (18–30), online and meme-literate, stressed by exams, work and deadlines. E.g. an MBA student at 11 PM with three assignments due who just wants to rant and laugh.
Your SolutionPick a parody persona (e.g. Dwight Schrute, Donald Trump, Gordon Ramsay, Sherlock Holmes, Miranda Priestly, Kanye West), vent by voice or text, and hear an in-character reply spoken back. Comedy makes stress feel smaller. Clearly labelled as entertainment, not real therapy.
User Flow
(how will it work for a user)		Open app → log in / sign up (optional) → pick persona → persona greets you (voice) → tap mic or type and vent → in-character reply (text + voice) → tap "End Session" → Session Prescription card (optional experience enhancement) → download / share, or try another persona.
UI
(Inspiration, style etc)		1. Pick your therapist: the 6 personas as cards, each in its own colour with its style tag. The selected card gets a border and plays its greeting. A "Parody" label sits at the bottom.
2. The couch: your rant on the right and the persona's reply on the left in its colour (e.g. Gordon in red), with live captions, a big pulsing mic, a type box and an "End session" button.
3. Session prescription: a doctor's-slip card with diagnosis, 3-step Rx, signoff and a tilted "PARODY" stamp, plus Download, Share and "Try another therapist".
Core features
(1-2 core features/functionalities)
  1. Voice chat with a parody persona: speak or type, and get an in-character reply read aloud in that persona's voice settings.
  2. Session Prescription card: the AI writes a diagnosis, 3-step Rx and signoff from your session, shown as a downloadable card.
Good to have features
(will build if time permits)		Login / sign-up page · download and share the Session Prescription card · compare your card with a friend · stress-level meter.
What you will not build
(eg payments, complex stuff)		Payments · real therapy or medical advice · native mobile app · multiplayer · admin panel · long-term storage of personal conversations.
Engineering
Will your product have a login? (Yes/No)No (an optional login is a good-to-have if time permits)
Will your product involve AI Models?Yes: one LLM through Emergent's built-in LLM integration, with a separate system prompt per persona, for chat replies and the prescription card.
Will your product involve external services?Only the browser's built-in Web Speech API (speech-to-text for the mic, text-to-speech for persona voices). No maps, email or payments.
